## Integrity statement

- No real radiation data were downloaded, parsed, or used.
- No citation was fabricated; bibliographic metadata was checked against Crossref for the
  cited works.
- No experiment result was fabricated or typed into the manuscript by hand. Tables, macros,
  and figures are generated by `scripts/aggregate_results.py` and `scripts/make_figures.py`
  from `results/raw/test_runs.csv` and the saved traces.
- No method, hyperparameter, threshold, or seed was changed after the held-out matrix was
  inspected. The two post-freeze additions (threshold-free AUC/event detection, and moving the
  raw files into `results/raw/`) are logged in `docs/protocol.md`.
- The oracle gate is labelled non-deployable everywhere it appears.
- The covariance-contamination mechanism is labelled as inferred, not isolated; no ablation
  was run.
- No claim of field readiness, operational safety, regulatory suitability, or novelty is made.

## Known weaknesses a reviewer will see

1. **No real data.** The study is a simulation of a simulation; the generator is not fitted to
   measured background.
2. **The protocol's operating point was infeasible.** No gated configuration met FAR <= 2/h on
   development data, so four of nine methods used the fallback rule. The resulting comparison
   is between blind methods and noisy ones, not at a common operating point.
3. **Confounded oracle comparison.** The oracle gate also selected a ten-fold smaller DAES
   gain, so its advantage cannot be attributed to gating alone.
4. **Mechanism inferred.** Adaptive-background contamination (mean and covariance) is supported
   by the static-versus-adaptive contrast and the traces, but a covariance-frozen or
   mean-frozen ablation would be needed to separate the two channels.
5. **Five seeds.** Intervals are wide and describe seed variation only.
6. **One representative trace.** The 89-versus-90 release near-miss is illustrative, not a
   distributional estimate.

## Recommended next actions (not blocking this draft)

1. Run a covariance-frozen ablation on the same test streams to isolate the contamination
   mechanism. This is a new experiment and should be reported separately.
2. Add a drift detector independent of the source detector, or a two-timescale covariance.
3. Re-evaluate at a matched false-alarm rate (threshold sweep) rather than one calibrated
   operating point.
4. Obtain a real or realistically calibrated stream before making any operational claim.
